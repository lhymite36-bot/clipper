Make an AI-voiceover recap of a film or trailer you have the rights to use. Short = 45–60s vertical with a hook bar; long = 16:9 with chapters. Music must be a file you own or a royalty-free / original bed — Clipper never rips audio from YouTube.

Important: a recap can still be claimed by Content ID even when the narration, cuts, and grade make it a commentary/review. Clipper does not flip, speed-shift, or otherwise try to hide a film from detection.

Publishing settings

      
Put BUFFER_API_KEY and CLIPPER_GITHUB_TOKEN in your .env (never paste them here). Buffer needs a public video URL; Clipper can upload the MP4 to a public GitHub repo or release, the same way Voice to Short does. Direct YouTube Data API upload is optional.
